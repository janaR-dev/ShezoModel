Shezo

Reality grounding · care team

When the room feels wrong, check what the camera actually saw.

Shezo reads your device's own camera and microphone summaries during a session you open, tells you only what they did or did not pick up, and sends the record to the clinician and family member assigned to you. It does not diagnose, and it never decides your experience for you.

Grounding checks, medication reminders, your care team.

Adherence, distress trend, escalations, report inbox.

Therapist

Patient record, session history, notes, medication plan.

Decision support, not a medical device. Nothing here replaces clinical judgement. If you might act on a thought, call your local emergency number — for example 988 (US), 111 (UK) or 112 (EU).
